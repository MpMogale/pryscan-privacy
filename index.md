<p class="tagline">Know the total before the till.</p>

Most of us find out what a shop actually cost at the checkout, when it is too
late to put anything back. Pryscan reads the price off the shelf label through
your camera and keeps a running total while you walk around.

## What it does

**Reads the shelf, not the receipt.** Point the camera at a price label and hold
still. Pryscan finds the price and adds it to the basket. Change the quantity
and the total follows.

**Keeps you inside a budget.** Set what you mean to spend and a bar shows where
you are — at the shelf, with the thing still in your hand, rather than at the
till with a queue behind you.

**Remembers the trip.** Close a basket and it is saved with the totals exactly
as they stood, so you can look back at what a big shop actually cost.

**Converts, when you are somewhere else.** If the price is in a currency you do
not think in, it converts as it reads. Rates are cached, so no signal is fine,
and you can set one by hand if it looks wrong.

**Works with no signal at all.** Text recognition runs on the phone, not on a
server.

**No account, no tracking.** Nothing to sign up for. Your baskets and trips are
on your phone and nowhere else.

## Getting it

Pryscan is in development for iOS. There is nothing to download yet.
