Support

Get in touch

Email is the fastest way to reach a person. Include the make of your phone and, if a price was misread, a photo of the shelf label — that is usually enough to find the problem.

pryscan@gmail.com

Replies usually within a few days.

Before you write

A price came out wrong
Hold the phone square to the label and fit the price inside the brackets. Shelf tags that show a unit price and a pack price together are the hardest case, and the one most worth reporting.
The total is converting at the wrong rate
Rates are downloaded and then cached so the app works offline. Pull to refresh on the basket, or set a rate by hand in Settings if you would rather fix it yourself.
The camera will not open
iOS asks for camera access once. If it was declined, turn it on under Settings → Pryscan. Prices can always be typed in by hand instead.
What does Pro cost?
Nothing yet — the paid tier is not on sale, and everything the app does today it does for free. When it opens it will be a single payment, with no subscription and no account, and a purchase will be restorable on any device signed in to the Apple ID that made it.
Where is my data?
On your phone, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes everything with it.
